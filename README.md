## Description
Based on the classic game [*Sokoban*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sokoban), a grid-based puzzle video game, turned into a first-person horror puzzle game about finding the key crate to unlock the exit. Taking place in the minimally lit [*Backrooms*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Backrooms), the player has to traverse through the map with only a flashlight, with a goal of finding the key crate and pushing it onto the keyhole tile.

Being in the dark is bad enough, but who said you were alone?

Four entities named "Ron", is roaming around and searching for you. Once you are spotted, your only options are to run and survive. Use your environment to your advantage by pushing crates to block pathways and shining your flashlight on the entity to temporarily freeze him in his tracks.

## Game Controls
- **W/A/S/D** - Move Forward/Left/Backward/Right by one tile (Player and Crates)
- **F** - Toggle Flashlight On/Off
- **Mouse Drag** - Look Around/Aim
- **Mouse Left Click** - Toggling Menu Buttons
